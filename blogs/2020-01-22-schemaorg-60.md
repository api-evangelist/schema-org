<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://schema.org/">Schema.org</a>&nbsp;version 6.0 has been released. See the&nbsp;<a href="https://schema.org/docs/releases.html#v6.0">release notes</a>&nbsp;for full details. &nbsp;As always, the release notes have full details and links (including previous releases e.g.&nbsp;<a href="https://schema.org/docs/releases.html#v5.0">5.0</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="https://schema.org/docs/releases.html#v4.0">4.0</a>).</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We are now aiming to release updated schemas on an approximately monthly basis (with longer gaps around vacation periods). Typically, new terms are first added to our &#8220;Pending&#8221; area to give time for the definitions to benefit from implementation experience before they are added to the &#8220;core&#8221; of Schema.org. As always, many thanks to everyone who has contributed to this release of Schema.org.</p>
