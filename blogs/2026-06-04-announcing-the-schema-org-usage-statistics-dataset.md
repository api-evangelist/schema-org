Google and the Schema.org community have released a new dataset providing aggregate usage statistics for Schema.org terms across the public web, updated monthly and available on the official Schema.org GitHub repository in CSV and JSON formats. The dataset counts are aggregated at the domain level and presented in popularity range buckets to maintain stability and respect privacy, and the statistics are now included directly on schema term pages. Other crawlers and indexers are invited to contribute their own statistics using the same open format.
